BEYOND CONVENTIONAL FASHION

ENGINEERING A MORE INTELLIGENT APPROACH TO FASHION.

Fashion technology is evolving beyond conventional garment design and manufacturing.

The convergence of computational intelligence, digital human modelling, dimensional measurement and emerging scientific research introduces new possibilities for understanding the human body, interpreting design preferences and developing personalised garment systems.

Green Wardrobe explores this intersection through specialised core technology domains alongside automated robotics fabrication and circular wardrobe renewal.
