UNDERSTANDING HUMAN GEOMETRY

THE HUMAN BODY, REPRESENTED DIGITALLY.

Human body geometry consists of complex spatial dimensions, proportions and anatomical relationships.

Traditional measurement methods generally capture selected dimensions individually. Three-dimensional scanning provides a different approach by capturing spatial information to construct a digital representation of the body.

This digital representation can serve as a reference for measurement analysis, body modelling and personalised garment development.

THE SCIENCE OF DIGITAL MEASUREMENT

FROM PHYSICAL DIMENSIONS TO DIGITAL GEOMETRY.

01

Data Acquisition

A scanning system captures spatial information from the human body using its supported sensing technology.

02

Point Cloud Generation

The captured spatial information can be represented as a collection of three-dimensional coordinates known as a point cloud.

03

3D Model Reconstruction

Spatial information is processed to generate a digital representation of the scanned body.

04

Measurement Extraction

Relevant dimensional measurements can be derived from the reconstructed model through appropriate measurement algorithms.

05

Digital Data Representation

The resulting measurements and geometry can be represented as structured digital information for subsequent applications.

FROM GEOMETRY TO APPLICATION

WHERE DIGITAL BODY MODELLING CAN BE APPLIED.

  • Body Measurement Analysis: Digital representation of individual body dimensions and proportions.
  • Custom Garment Development: Using measurement information as an input for personalised garment specifications.
  • Virtual Body Representation: Creating digital human models for visualisation and computational applications.
  • Dimensional Data Management: Organising measurement information into structured digital records.
  • Fit Analysis: Exploring garment-to-body dimensional relationships through digital modelling.
