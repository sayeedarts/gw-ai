UNDERSTANDING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

BEYOND PROGRAMMED INSTRUCTIONS.

Artificial intelligence refers to computational systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human-like cognitive capabilities, including interpretation, classification, prediction and content generation.

In digital fashion applications, AI can provide a framework for processing different forms of information, understanding user-defined preferences and exploring relationships between design elements.

Its application extends beyond simple automation into intelligent interpretation and computational decision support.

THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER

DIFFERENT APPROACHES. CONNECTED CAPABILITIES.

Machine Perception

The ability of computational systems to interpret information from visual and other sensory inputs.

  • Image Recognition & Object Detection
  • Visual Classification & Image Segmentation

Computer Vision

Computational techniques for extracting meaningful information from digital images and video.

  • Visual Analysis & Image Processing
  • Human Shape Analysis & Object Recognition

Generative AI

AI approaches that generate new content based on learned patterns and supplied inputs.

  • Image Generation & Design Exploration
  • Visual Concept Development & Modelling

Recommendation Systems

Computational approaches for identifying and suggesting relevant options based on input information and defined criteria.

  • Personalised Recommendations & Preference-Based Filtering
  • Similarity Analysis & Recommendation Ranking

Natural Language Processing

Methods that enable computational systems to interpret and process human language.

  • Text Interpretation & Intent Recognition
  • Conversational Interfaces & Language Input Processing

APPLIED INTELLIGENCE

INTERPRETING THE MANY DIMENSIONS OF STYLE.

  • Style Brief Interpretation: Processing written descriptions and preferences to identify relevant styling requirements.
  • Visual Style Analysis: Analysing visual references and identifying relevant clothing and design characteristics.
  • Design Generation: Exploring computational generation of visual concepts and outfit possibilities.
  • Contextual Recommendations: Considering occasion, preferences and other supplied information when developing recommendations.
  • Digital Look Visualisation: Exploring AI-assisted visual representations of proposed styling concepts.

AI AND MACHINE LEARNING

TWO RELATED TECHNOLOGIES. DIFFERENT ROLES.

Artificial intelligence is the broader field concerned with creating systems capable of intelligent tasks.

Machine learning is a major area within AI that develops models through data-driven learning rather than relying exclusively on explicitly programmed rules.

AI defines the broader intelligent-system objective. ML provides important techniques for developing systems that learn patterns from data.
