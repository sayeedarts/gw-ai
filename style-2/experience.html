STEP-BY-STEP ARCHITECTURE

FROM DIGITAL SCAN TO SIGNATURE LOOK.

Scroll or select any stage below to inspect how physical proportions, style preferences and automated tailoring align.

01

3D + ML Screening

  • 3D spatial body capture maps posture, silhouette and dimensional symmetry.
  • Replaces standard S/M/L sizing racks with individual human geometry.
02

Robotics Measurement

  • Structured anatomical coordinates — height, neck, shoulder, chest, sleeve, waist, hip and inseam.
  • Translates body metrics into exact digital garment pattern tolerances.
03

ML Fabric Selection

  • Machine learning curation matches cloth weight, weave, drape and palette to occasion and climate.
  • Pairs tactile fabric swatches with your bespoke silhouette requirements.
04

ML + AI Styling

  • Interprets your brief across Western formal suits, blazers and Traditional Indian attire.
  • Visualises full proportions before cutting begins — see it before you wear it.
05

ML + AI Accessory Selection

  • Balances lapels, footwear, timepieces, pocket squares and understated metal accents.
  • Harmonises textures and colours with your primary garment selection.
06

Hair + Grooming — ML

  • Completes the presentation with facial-frame, collar-line and grooming guidance.
  • Ensures silhouette and personal presentation read cohesively for the occasion.
07

Automated Cutting, Precise Stitching through ML

  • Computer-guided laser cutting prepares each panel straight from your digital body profile.
  • Precision robotics-assisted stitching reinforces seams and embroidery consistency.
08

Final Result

  • The finished bespoke wardrobe piece — fitted to your exact movement and posture.
  • Supported by ReWardrobe renewal: return eligible pre-loved garments for up to 25% off new purchases (terms apply).
